An Apple TV screen is not a phone held at arm's length. People are several meters away, and the experience may remain in the room for a long time.

## Design for distance first

Small labels and dense controls disappear quickly. Keep important information large, reduce the number of focus stops, and make the selected state unmistakable.

## Motion should stay in the background

- Avoid fast transitions that repeatedly pull attention.
- Consider how long static elements remain in one position.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and in-app effect controls.

Music can enrich the room without turning the screen into a dense player interface. Keep only the information that supports the atmosphere.
